// B3 · arrange and save

A world builder for the EDGE platform: browse the game's assets, drop them into the world, position, arrange, bake.

To place an asset: pick it, then Drop where I look, or Drop at click and click the spot in the world (Esc or right-click cancels). On the Assets tab, typing searches.

Switch Edit on (top of this window) to work in the world: click an object to select it, drag an arrow to move it along that axis, drag the ring to turn it. Keys: G move, R turn, S scale (meshes), then X, Y or Z to lock an axis and a number to type the amount; Enter or a click keeps it, Esc or a right-click undoes it. Ctrl snaps, Ctrl+E drops to the floor, Delete removes. With Edit off the game gets the mouse as usual.

With Edit on, EDGEbuilder looks through a camera of its own and hides the game's HUD; V stays where V is. Hold the right mouse button to look around and fly with W A S D, Q down, E up (Shift faster, the wheel changes the speed while flying). The middle mouse orbits the selected object, Shift with it pans, the wheel moves closer, F frames the selection. Switch Edit off and the view is V's again. Both can be switched off in Settings, also while Edit is on.

To arrange: Ctrl+click adds an object to the selection, Shift+drag selects everything in a box, and a move, turn or scale then acts on all of them, around their middle. Ctrl+G groups the selection and Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups; a click on an object in a group selects the group, Alt+click just that object. Ctrl+D duplicates the selection, which then follows the mouse until a click places it (Esc or right-click: no copy). Ctrl+C copies the selection, Ctrl+X cuts it (copies, then deletes), and Ctrl+V pastes it where the cursor points, standing on the surface there, as often as you like. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes, Clear scene included; Undo, Redo, Copy, Group and Ungroup are buttons on the Scene tab too. Groups open and close with the arrow.

Scenes: the open scene is saved after every change, and Close takes its objects out of the world with no scene open; Ctrl+S confirms it and Ctrl+Shift+S saves a copy under a new name. Its name is at the top of the Scene tab (Enter renames it). Objects on the Library tab lists a scene's objects beside this window: two clicks fly the camera to one and select it, and its trash icon deletes it wherever it is. The Library tab lists every scene: in the Library (your own folders, shared by the EDGE tools; set it in Settings), in the open REDcode project, and on This PC. Pick one to open it, or Show in world to see it beside the one you are editing (shown, not editable). EDGEbuilder opens the last scene again when it starts.

Bake: the Bake tab writes the open scene into the open REDcode project, as a streaming sector of its own. Pack and install the project, and it stands in the world from the next game start, with EDGEbuilder off. Its previews then stay off, so nothing stands twice.
